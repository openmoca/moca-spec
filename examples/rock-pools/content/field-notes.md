# Field notes: one morning in March

Low water at 07:40. Cold, bright, no wind. The pools at the north end of the
bay are deeper than they look; the biggest is the length of a bathtub and
twice as deep.

Counted eleven beadlet anemones in that one pool, most of them closed. Three
were open in the shade under the overhang, red as cherries.

A shore crab backed into a crack and held up both claws at me. I took the
hint.

Under the weed on the seaward side: a blenny, perfectly still, the same
brown as the rock. I only saw it when it blinked.

By 08:30 the first wave was over the outer ledge. Time to go.

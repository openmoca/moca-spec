# At a glance

1. Rock pools are pockets of seawater left on a rocky shore by the falling
   tide.
2. Go at low tide, and check a tide table for your beach before you set out.
3. Wear shoes with a good grip and step on bare rock, not weed.
4. Look closely, then put back every stone and animal as you found it.
5. Watch the tide, and leave before it turns.

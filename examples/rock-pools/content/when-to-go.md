# When to go

Go at low tide. The pools are only there to look at when the sea has gone
out, and the lowest tides of the month uncover the most. Look up a tide table
for your beach before you leave, and arrive an hour or so before low water,
so you have time on the rocks while the tide is still going out.

Watch the tide, not the clock. When it turns, it can come in faster than you
expect, and it can cut you off on a rock that was dry when you walked out.
Agree a time to head back, and keep to it.

## What to take

- Shoes with a good grip. Step on bare rock: it is less slippery than rock
  covered in weed, and you crush less of what lives there.
- A clear tub or a white tray, if you want a closer look. Fill it with water
  from the pool, look, and pour it back gently in the same place.
- A bag for any litter you find.

Leave the net at home. If you lift a stone, put it back the way it was, the
same way up, and leave the animals and plants where they are.

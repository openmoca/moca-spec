# Words you will meet

**Anemone.** A soft animal fixed to the rock by its base. Open, it is a ring
of tentacles; closed, a blob of jelly.

**Intertidal zone.** The part of the shore covered by the sea at high tide and
uncovered at low tide. Rock pools are found here.

**Low water.** The lowest point the tide reaches before it turns. Tide tables
give its time for each day.

**Spring tide.** A tide with a bigger range than usual, twice a month, near
the new and full moon. It has nothing to do with the season.

**Neap tide.** A tide with a smaller range than usual, about a week after a
spring tide.

**Wrack.** The tough brown seaweed that covers the rocks at low tide.

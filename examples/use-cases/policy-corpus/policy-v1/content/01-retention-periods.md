# Retention periods (v1)

> **Superseded.** This version was in force from 2024-01-01 to 2026-06-30.
> It remains readable and independently valid so that decisions taken while it
> applied can still be audited against the text that actually governed them.

Customer transaction records are retained for **7 years** from the date of the
final transaction. Application log data is retained for **90 days**.

# Retention periods (v2)

Customer transaction records are retained for **6 years** from the date of the
final transaction. Application log data is retained for **30 days**.

Both periods were shortened from the v1 policy. A system answering "what is
our retention period?" must return these values, and must not blend them with
the superseded v1 figures.

# Chapter Summary

From Chapter 8: Airport Traffic Patterns, page 8-7 ([PDF](<https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/airplane_handbook/09_afh_ch8.pdf#page=7>)).

The volume of traffic at an airport can create a hazardous environment. Airport traffic patterns are procedures that improve the flow of traffic at an airport and enhance safety when properly executed. Most reported mid-air collisions occur during the final or shortfinal approach leg of the airport traffic pattern.

# Shipping estimates

| Region | Standard | Express |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Domestic | 3-5 working days | 1-2 working days |
| Europe | 5-8 working days | 2-4 working days |

Quote the range, never a single day. For stock issues see
[Refund eligibility window](refund-window.md).

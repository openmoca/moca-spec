# The recipe card

One loaf, about 800 g baked.

| Ingredient | Amount | Baker's percentage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strong white flour | 500 g | 100% |
| Water, lukewarm | 340 g | 68% |
| Salt | 10 g | 2% |
| Dried yeast | 7 g | 1.4% |

## Timings

- Mix: 5 minutes. Rest: 10 minutes.
- Knead: 10 minutes by hand.
- First rise: 1 to 2 hours, until doubled.
- Shape, then second rise: 45 minutes.
- Bake: 35 minutes at 230 °C, with steam for the first 10.

# Lesson 2: kneading and rising

**Kneading** builds the gluten. Push the dough away with the heel of your
hand, fold it back, turn it a quarter, and repeat. After about ten minutes it
turns smooth and springs back when you press it.

The **windowpane test** tells you when to stop. Stretch a small piece between
your fingers. If it goes thin enough to let light through without tearing,
the gluten is ready.

**The first rise.** Put the dough in a clean bowl, cover it, and leave it
somewhere warm until it has doubled: one to two hours. A cool kitchen is not
a problem. The rise takes longer, and the bread tastes better for it.

Press a floured finger into the risen dough. If the dent fills back slowly,
it is ready to shape.

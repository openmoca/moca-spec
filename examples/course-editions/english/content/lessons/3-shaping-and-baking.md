# Lesson 3: shaping and baking

**Shaping.** Tip the dough onto a lightly floured surface. Fold the edges
into the middle, turn it over, and draw it towards you so the top tightens
into a smooth ball. Put it on a tray, seam down, and cover it for the second
rise, about 45 minutes.

**The oven.** Heat it to 230 °C with an empty tin on the lowest shelf. Just
before the loaf goes in, slash the top with a sharp knife, then pour a cup of
hot water into the tin. The steam keeps the crust soft for the first minutes,
so the loaf can open.

**Baking.** Bake for about 35 minutes. The loaf is done when it is deep brown
and sounds hollow when you tap the bottom.

**Cooling.** Leave it on a rack for an hour before you cut it. The inside is
still cooking as it cools.

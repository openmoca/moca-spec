# Deleting a customer account

> **This node is marked `disputed` on purpose.** Legal and Customer Operations
> have not agreed the retention period that applies after deletion, so a
> harness should not present this as settled guidance.

Customers can request account deletion from Settings. What happens to order
history after deletion is the point still under discussion.
